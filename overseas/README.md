# MD+ — a proper reading surface for the files your phone throws at you

[English] | [中文](../README.md)

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-2.4.1-blue) ![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-Android%207.0%2B-green) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-orange) ![Offline](https://img.shields.io/badge/offline-zero%20permissions-brightgreen)

MD+ is an offline document reader for Android. It opens **Markdown, TXT, PDF, EPUB and Word files** in one clean, focused view — with highlights, full-text search, a table of contents and reading themes.

No ads. No analytics. No account. And no permissions at all — **not even network access**.

> **Status:** the app UI is currently Chinese-only. English localization is the next milestone. Everything below describes what you'll get today; the reader itself is fully usable regardless of UI language.

## Why it exists

Documents arrive on your phone from everywhere — WhatsApp, Telegram, email attachments, your Downloads folder. There's no good place to *read* them: Markdown isn't rendered anywhere, long PDFs open in whatever viewer happens to be installed, nothing remembers where you stopped, and you can't highlight a line without a subscription.

MD+ fixes exactly that, and does nothing else.

## Download

Get the latest APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/billmax99/mdp/releases/latest) (arm64 — works on virtually every Android phone from the last several years, ~25 MB).

The first install will ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. That's the standard Android sideloading prompt, nothing unusual.

## Features

- **Five formats, one reader** — Markdown (fully rendered), TXT, PDF, EPUB e-books, Word (.docx)
- **Highlight & underline** — long-press any passage; wavy underlines and highlight blocks are saved per document, permanently
- **Full-text search** — see every hit at a glance and jump straight to the third occurrence
- **Table of contents** — chapter navigation for long documents and e-books
- **Picks up where you left off** — every file remembers its own reading position
- **Six reading themes** — four eye-friendly palettes, light/dark following the system, adjustable font size

## Opening files from other apps

- **WhatsApp / Telegram** — tap the document, then pick MD+ from the *Open with* sheet
- **Email & Downloads** — open the attachment or file and choose MD+
- **From inside MD+** — use the picker button on the home screen

## Privacy — the short version

MD+ requests zero Android permissions, including network. It physically cannot send your data anywhere. Documents you open, your annotations and your preferences live only in the app's private storage; uninstalling removes every trace.

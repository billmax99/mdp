# MD+ Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-18 · Version v1.1-en

## The short version

**MD+ collects nothing.** The app requests zero Android permissions — including network access — so it is technically incapable of sending any data off your device. No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no push services.

## What stays on your device

All of the following live only in the app's private storage and are deleted entirely when you uninstall the app:

- **Reading preferences** — font size, theme, light/dark setting
- **Annotations** — the highlights and underlines you make in documents
- **Recent files list** — file names you opened, and when
- **Reading positions** — where you stopped, per file
- **Document copies** — private copies of files you opened or imported

## File access

MD+ only ever touches files you explicitly hand it: through the system file picker (SAF), or when another app passes a document to it (*Open with* from WhatsApp, Telegram, email, your file manager, etc.). It never scans, indexes, or accesses other files in the background.

## Children

MD+ is not directed at children — and since it collects no one's data, children's data included, there is nothing more to say here.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the change will be called out in the release notes. Changes never retroactively alter how data was handled before.

## Your rights

There is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes every local trace — documents, annotations, preferences — with no cloud residue.

For anything else, contact the developer: **mdp.supporter+mdp@gmail.com** (answered within 15 business days).

## Verify it yourself

The "no network permission" claim is checkable: open the app's permission list in Android Settings, or decompile the APK. We mean it literally.

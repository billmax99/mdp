A proper reading surface for the files your phone throws at you

Markdown, TXT, PDF, EPUB and Word — one clean, focused reader.

Fully offline Zero permissions No ads · no IAP Android 7.0+
Download on GitHub

arm64 APK, ~25 MB — works on virtually every Android phone from the last several years. The first install asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app: that's the standard Android sideloading prompt.

Why MD+

Documents arrive on your phone from everywhere — WhatsApp, Telegram, email attachments, your Downloads folder. There's no good place to read them: Markdown isn't rendered anywhere, long PDFs open in whatever viewer happens to be installed, nothing remembers where you stopped, and you can't highlight a line. MD+ fixes exactly that, and does nothing else.

Heads-up: the app UI is currently Chinese-only; English localization is the next milestone. The reader itself is fully usable regardless of UI language.

Features

Five formats

Markdown (fully rendered) / TXT / PDF / EPUB (with images) / Word .docx

Highlight & underline

Long-press any passage — wavy underlines or highlight blocks, saved per document

Full-text search

Every hit highlighted, n/m counter, jump through matches one by one

Table of contents

Markdown headings and EPUB chapters, one tap away

Picks up where you left off

Every file remembers its own reading position

Reading themes

Six palettes including true dark, follows the system; adjustable font size

Opening files

WhatsApp / Telegram — tap the document, then pick MD+ from the Open with sheet.
Email & Downloads — open the attachment or file and choose MD+.
From inside MD+ — use the picker button on the home screen.

Privacy

MD+ requests zero Android permissions — including network access. Technically, no data can ever leave your device: no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no push services. Everything lives in the app's private storage; uninstalling removes all of it.
